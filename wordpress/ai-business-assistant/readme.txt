Embed your intelligent AI business assistant trained on your own documents and knowledge base.

== Description ==

AI Business Assistant allows you to connect your WordPress website to your trained AI chatbot in seconds.
Features:
- Train chatbot on PDF, CSV, JSON, DOCX, and TXT files.
- $0 mandatory API cost (powered by open-source embeddings and local Ollama or cloud models).
- Stream responses in real time with source citations.
- Floating chat bubble with customizable colors and position.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `ai-business-assistant` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Go to 'AI Assistant' in the WordPress admin menu.
4. Enter your Assistant Host URL and Assistant ID.
5. Save changes — your chatbot is now live!
