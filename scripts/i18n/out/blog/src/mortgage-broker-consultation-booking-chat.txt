P: People approaching a mortgage broker are often anxious, confused by jargon and comparing several firms. They want to know whether a broker can help them, how the process works, what documents they will need and whether they qualify, and they want to speak to a person soon. A website chat can meet them at the moment they search, explain how you work in plain language and book an initial consultation. But mortgage advice is regulated. A chatbot must not recommend products, estimate approval or say what someone can borrow. This guide explains how a broker can use chat responsibly: what it can answer, what it must never do, how to word the disclosure, how to capture an enquiry and how to book a consultation that starts with the right information.
NOTE: Mortgage and financial advice are regulated, and rules differ by country. This is general information, not legal or compliance advice. Have your compliance officer approve all wording, and check what your regulator requires for automated communications.
H2[what-people-ask-a-mortgage-broker]: What people ask a mortgage broker
TABLE:
H: Question | What the chat can do
R: How does a broker help? | Explain your role and how you are paid, in the words you approve
R: What documents do I need? | Share a checklist
R: How long does it take? | Give typical stages, without promising timelines
R: Can I get a mortgage with a deposit of X? | Not answer; offer a consultation
R: What is the best rate? | Not answer; explain you compare options at the consultation
R: Do you charge a fee? | State your fee structure as approved
R: Can I speak to someone today? | Offer real consultation times
H2[the-boundary-information-not-advice]: The boundary: information, not advice
TABLE:
H: The assistant can | The assistant must not
R: Explain your process and typical steps | Recommend a product or lender
R: List documents commonly needed | Say whether a specific person will be approved
R: Explain general terms such as deposit or fixed rate in neutral language | Estimate how much someone can borrow
R: State your fee and remuneration as approved | Quote rates unless approved and current
R: Book a consultation | Discuss a customer's individual finances in detail
P: Write these lines into its instructions and test them with tempting questions such as 'Can I afford a $600,000 home on $80,000?'
H2[disclosure-and-tone]: Disclosure and tone
UL:
- Say clearly that it is an AI assistant, and that it cannot give advice.
- Say that advice is given by qualified people at the consultation.
- Include any required statements about your status, regulator and how you are paid, as your compliance officer decides.
- Keep the tone warm, reassuring and plain: mortgages are stressful.
P: Example: 'I am an AI assistant. I can explain how we work and arrange a free consultation with one of our advisers. I cannot give mortgage advice or say what you could borrow.' See [AI transparency and disclosure](/blog/ai-transparency-disclosure-building-trust).
H2[what-to-capture-in-the-chat]: What to capture in the chat
TABLE:
H: Field | Why
R: Name and preferred contact method | To reach them
R: Type of enquiry: first-time buyer, remortgage, buy-to-let, other | Route to the right adviser
R: Rough timeframe | Priority
R: Whether they have found a property | Urgency
R: Preferred consultation time | Booking
P: Do not ask for income, credit details, national identifiers or bank information in an open chat. Say that the adviser will ask what is needed during a secure process. See the [GDPR checklist for website chatbots](/blog/website-chatbot-gdpr-checklist).
H2[booking-the-consultation]: Booking the consultation
OL:
- Explain what the consultation is: length, whether free, and what the adviser will cover.
- State what the client should have ready: recent payslips or accounts, identification, details of the property if known.
- Offer real times, including evenings if you have them.
- Confirm by email with the adviser's name, how the call or meeting works and how to change it.
- Send a reminder the day before.

For advisers with different diaries, use Calendly or Cal.com links per adviser. H2[handle-urgency-and-complex-cases]: Handle urgency and complex cases
UL:
- A buyer with an offer accepted or a deadline: alert an adviser quickly.
- Self-employed, adverse credit or unusual income: do not comment; book a consultation and note the case type.
- Someone in financial difficulty: respond with care and offer to connect them with a person, and, where relevant, to independent support services.
H2[a-sample-conversation]: A sample conversation
TABLE:
H: Speaker | Message
R: Visitor | I have a deposit saved and want to buy my first home. What mortgage can I get?
R: Assistant | Congratulations on getting this far. I am an AI assistant, so I cannot say what you could borrow or recommend a mortgage, but one of our advisers can look at your situation in a free 30-minute call. They will explain your options and next steps. Would you like to see times this week?
R: Visitor | Yes please, evening if possible.
R: Assistant | I can offer Wednesday at 6:30 p.m. or Thursday at 7:00 p.m. What name and phone number should the adviser use? Please do not share income or financial details here; they will ask when you speak.
H2[measure-it]: Measure it
TABLE:
H: Metric | Why
R: Enquiries and consultations booked | Conversion
R: Out-of-hours bookings | Coverage
R: Consultation no-show rate | Reminder quality
R: Consultations to applications | Lead quality
R: Compliance reviews of transcripts | Risk control
P: Review transcripts regularly with your compliance lead for wording that drifts into advice.
H2[a-pre-consultation-checklist-to-send]: A pre-consultation checklist to send
P: A consultation goes better when the client arrives prepared. Include a short checklist in the confirmation email, with wording approved by your compliance lead.
UL:
- Recent payslips or accounts, as your firm requires.
- Identification documents.
- Details of existing debts and commitments, if relevant.
- The property details, if a property has been found.
- Questions the client wants to ask.
- Who else should attend, for example a partner.
P: Keep it short, warm and clear, and say that nothing needs to be sent through the chat.
H2[after-the-consultation]: After the consultation
OL:
- Send a written summary and next steps, as your rules require.
- Record the outcome in your system.
- Follow up if documents are outstanding.
- Ask for feedback and, where permitted, reviews after completion.
P: The chat's job ends with a well-informed client in front of an adviser. Everything after that is your process, and the quality of the first conversation makes it smoother.
H2[common-mistakes]: Common mistakes
UL:
- Letting the assistant hint at what someone could borrow.
- Quoting rates that are out of date.
- Asking for income or credit details in the chat.
- Skipping the disclosure that it is an AI.
- Not reviewing transcripts for advice creeping in.
- Making the consultation hard to book at evenings and weekends.
H2[keep-clients-informed-between-steps]: Keep clients informed between steps
P: Mortgage applications take weeks, and silence breeds anxiety. Agree how you will keep clients updated, for example a short message at each stage, and make sure the assistant can tell a client who to contact for a status update. Clients who feel informed are more likely to complete and to recommend you.
H2[frequently-asked-questions]: Frequently asked questions
FAQ:
Q: Can a chatbot give mortgage advice?
A: No. It can explain your process and arrange a consultation, but advice must come from qualified people under the rules of your regulator.
Q: Can it say what someone can borrow?
A: No. That is advice or an estimate that should come from an adviser after assessing the person's circumstances.
Q: What should it collect in chat?
A: Name, contact method, enquiry type and timeframe. Not income, credit or identity details.
Q: Can it quote rates?
A: Only if your compliance officer approves and the rates are current. Many firms prefer not to.
Q: How do I keep it compliant?
A: Have compliance write and approve the wording, test tempting questions and review transcripts regularly.
Q: Can it book consultations?
A: Yes, with available times and an email confirmation.
