P: Bring your own key, usually shortened to BYOK, is a simple idea with a slightly technical name. When an AI chatbot answers a visitor, a language model does the writing, and that model belongs to a provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq or DeepSeek. The provider charges for every reply. In a BYOK product, you create your own account with the provider, paste your key into the chatbot platform and the provider bills you directly for what your assistant uses. The platform charges only for its software. This guide explains how BYOK works in plain terms, what you gain and what you take on, how keys are kept safe, how to pick a provider and model, what it costs, how to set it up step by step and when a bundled plan may suit you better. It also explains how Crescelo does it: no markup on AI usage, and keys stored encrypted.
H2[the-idea-in-one-picture]: The idea in one picture
TABLE:
H:  | Bundled AI | Bring your own key
R: You pay the platform for | Software and AI together | Software only
R: Who pays the AI provider | The platform, out of your fee | You, directly
R: Who chooses the model | The platform | You
R: Where the margin is | In the price of the bundle | Nowhere on AI: you pay the provider's rate
R: What you set up | Nothing extra | An account with a provider and a key
P: A key is a long secret string, like a password for software. It lets the platform ask the provider to write replies on your account.
H2[why-people-choose-byok]: Why people choose BYOK
UL:
- **Visible cost:** the provider's dashboard shows exactly what each day of chats costs.
- **No markup:** you pay wholesale rates instead of a bundle's margin.
- **Choice and change:** you can pick a model and switch when a better or cheaper one appears.
- **Privacy control:** you decide which provider sees your visitors' messages, and can pick one whose terms suit you.
- **Existing accounts and credits:** if you already have an account or credits, you can use them.
P: See [BYOK versus bundled pricing](/blog/byok-vs-bundled-ai-pricing-cost-comparison) for a worked cost model.
H2[what-you-take-on]: What you take on
UL:
- Creating and managing an account with a provider, including payment.
- Watching usage and setting spending limits so a surprise bill cannot happen.
- Choosing a model, and revisiting the choice.
- Reading the provider's terms about data and privacy.
- Keeping the key secret and rotating it if it might have leaked.
P: None of this is difficult, and each step takes minutes, but it is more than a bundled plan asks of you.
H2[how-the-key-is-used-and-protected]: How the key is used and protected
OL:
- You paste the key into the platform's AI settings once.
- The platform encrypts it before storing it. Crescelo stores provider keys encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
- When a visitor asks a question, the server decrypts the key in memory, sends the request to the provider and discards the plaintext.
- The key is never sent to the visitor's browser and never appears in your website code.
P: Good practice on your side: create a key just for this assistant, give it only the permissions it needs, set a monthly spending limit at the provider and revoke and replace the key if a team member with access leaves. See [how a bring-your-own-key vault works](/blog/cryptographic-byok-vault-aes-256-gcm) for the technical detail.

H2[what-it-costs-in-practice]: What it costs in practice
P: For a grounded assistant answering from your documents, the AI cost per conversation is usually a fraction of a cent to a few cents on a small or mid-range model. A small business with a few hundred conversations a month typically sees AI usage in the low single or double digits of dollars, while the platform subscription is the larger part of the bill. Premium models cost many times more for little gain on simple questions, which is why testing a cheaper model is worthwhile. The cost is visible in your provider's dashboard, so you can check it against the conversations you had.
H2[setup-in-five-steps]: Setup in five steps
OL:
- Choose a provider and create an account; add a payment method or credits.
- Create a new API key named for this assistant.
- Set a monthly spending limit or alert at the provider if offered.
- Paste the key into the assistant's AI settings in the platform.
- Test with twenty real questions, including some the documents cannot answer, and watch the provider's usage page.
P: The provider guides in the help centre walk through each provider: [OpenAI](/support/ai-connectors/openai), [Gemini](/support/ai-connectors/gemini) and [the others](/support/ai-connectors).
H2[privacy-where-your-visitors-messages-go]: Privacy: where your visitors' messages go
P: With BYOK, the platform sends the visitor's message and the retrieved passages from your documents to the provider you chose, under your account and that provider's terms. Read those terms: whether API data is stored, for how long, whether it is used for training and where it is processed. Many providers do not train on API data by default for business use, but confirm. Tell visitors they are talking to an AI and link to your privacy policy. See the [GDPR checklist for website chatbots](/blog/website-chatbot-gdpr-checklist).
H2[when-a-bundled-plan-may-suit-you-better]: When a bundled plan may suit you better
UL:
- You want the simplest setup and never to think about a provider account.
- Your volume is very low and predictable.
- You prefer one invoice.
- Nobody on your team is comfortable managing keys and limits.
P: Both models are legitimate. BYOK is best when you value visibility, choice and no markup, and are willing to spend ten minutes on setup.
H2[common-mistakes]: Common mistakes
UL:
- Sharing one key across several projects.
- Forgetting a spending limit.
- Choosing the most expensive model by default.
- Pasting the key into a chat, email or website code.
- Never checking usage.
- Not reading the provider's data terms.
H2[frequently-asked-questions]: Frequently asked questions
FAQ:
Q: What does bring your own key mean?
A: You connect your own account with an AI provider to the chatbot platform, and the provider bills you directly for AI usage while the platform charges only for its software.
Q: Is BYOK cheaper?
A: Often, especially as volume grows or when a small model is enough, because there is no margin on AI usage. At very low volume the difference may be small.
Q: Is my API key safe?
A: It should be stored encrypted, never sent to visitors and kept out of your website code. Use a dedicated key and set spending limits.
Q: Which providers does Crescelo support?
A: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek and self-hosted endpoints, plus a free engine for testing.
Q: Can I change providers later?
A: Yes. It is a settings change. Retest your questions after switching.
Q: Do I need technical skills?
A: No. You create a key in the provider's dashboard and paste it into the settings.
