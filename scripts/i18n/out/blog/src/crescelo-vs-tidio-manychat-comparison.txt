P: Tidio and ManyChat are popular, and they are built for different things. Tidio-style products combine live chat, a shared inbox and chatbots, often with a focus on online shops. ManyChat-style products automate conversations on social platforms, such as Instagram and Messenger, mainly for marketing. If you run a service business and are comparing them with an AI assistant built around appointments, it helps to see where each approach shines.
P: We build Crescelo, so treat this as an informed but interested comparison. Products change quickly: check each vendor's current features and prices before deciding. We describe categories and approaches, not competitors' prices.
H2[three-different-starting-points]: Three different starting points
TABLE:
H: Product type | Started as | Center of gravity
R: Live chat suite with chatbots (Tidio-style) | A live chat widget and inbox for humans | Agents chatting with visitors, with automation and AI added
R: Social messaging automation (ManyChat-style) | Marketing flows on Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and similar | Campaigns, keywords, sequences and broadcasts
R: AI assistant for service businesses (Crescelo) | Answering from your content and booking appointments | Grounded answers, booking, lead capture and handover
P: A product's starting point shapes its strengths. Live chat suites are excellent for teams who will staff the chat. Social automation is excellent for growing an audience and running campaigns. An assistant built around appointments is strongest where the visitor's goal is to ask a question and book a time.
H2[where-a-live-chat-suite-fits]: Where a live chat suite fits
UL:
- You have people who will answer chats during business hours.
- You want a strong shared inbox with roles, canned replies and visitor information.
- You run an online shop and want cart recovery and product recommendations.
- You want automation as a layer on top of human chat.
P: The trade-off for a small service business is staffing and cost. If nobody is free to answer, a chat suite's human features go unused, and some suites price per seat or per conversation. See [the real cost of staffing live chat](/blog/live-chat-hidden-costs-small-business) and [Crescelo vs traditional live chat](/blog/crescelo-vs-traditional-live-chat).
H2[where-social-automation-fits]: Where social automation fits
UL:
- Your customers find you on Instagram or Facebook and message you there.
- You run campaigns with comment keywords, giveaways or lead magnets.
- You want to build an audience list and send sequences.
- You are comfortable designing flows and maintaining them.
P: The trade-off is that flow-based automation follows a script. A visitor who asks something you did not draw a branch for meets a dead end, and knowledge-heavy questions, such as detailed prices and policies, are hard to serve well with a tree. See [rule-based chatbots vs AI assistants](/blog/rule-based-vs-ai-chatbots).
H2[where-an-appointment-focused-ai-assistant-fits]: Where an appointment-focused AI assistant fits
UL:
- Visitors ask open questions about services, prices and policies, and you want answers from your own content.
- You want visitors to book a time without leaving the chat.
- You want leads captured and delivered to you instantly, with the conversation.
- You want a person to be able to step in with the full context.
- You want to control AI cost by connecting your own provider.
P: It fits the person who thinks: 'I want my website to answer questions and fill my calendar while I am busy.'

H2[pricing-patterns-to-compare]: Pricing patterns to compare
OL:
- **Per seat:** a fee for each team member. Costs rise as your team grows.
- **Per conversation or resolution:** fees scale with usage. Good for low volume, less predictable at high volume.
- **Contact-based:** fees scale with the size of your audience list. Common in social automation.
- **Platform plus your own AI key:** a subscription with included sessions, and you pay the AI provider directly (Crescelo).
P: Ask each vendor for the total price at your current volume and at three times that. Crescelo's plans are $49, $149 and $399 a month; see the [pricing page](/pricing) for what each includes.

H2[using-them-together]: Using them together
P: These tools are not always alternatives. A business can use a social automation tool for Instagram campaigns and an AI assistant on its website to answer questions and book. It can use a live chat inbox for staffed hours and an assistant for the rest. The important thing is that each visitor gets a clear route to an answer or a booking, and that you know where each lead came from. See [attribution for chat leads](/blog/attribution-modeling-marketing-channels-chat-leads).
H2[example-a-physiotherapy-clinic-deciding-between-the-three]: Example: a physiotherapy clinic deciding between the three
P: A clinic gets most enquiries from its website, some from Instagram and a few from returning patients by phone. The owner wants fewer missed enquiries and more bookings, but has no one free to sit on chat all day.
TABLE:
H: Option | What it does well for the clinic | What it does not solve
R: Live chat suite | Lets the team chat with visitors when someone is free | Nobody is free most of the day, so the chat goes unanswered unless automation covers it
R: Social automation | Answers Instagram DMs with keyword flows and captures contacts | Detailed questions about treatments and prices, and booking on the website
R: AI assistant for appointments | Answers website questions from the clinic's documents and books slots | Instagram campaigns and comment automation
P: The clinic chooses an AI assistant for the website, keeps its simple Instagram auto-replies and adds a handover so the team can step in. Three tools, each doing what it is best at.
H2[questions-to-ask-about-staffing]: Questions to ask about staffing
UL:
- Who will answer chats, and when? If the answer is 'nobody, mostly', choose automation first.
- Do I want to reply in real time, or capture and follow up later?
- How will my team be notified when a visitor needs a person?
- Can I see the full history when I take over?
- What does it cost if two more people join my team?
H2[what-the-transition-looks-like]: What the transition looks like
OL:
- Export any existing chat transcripts and leads.
- Gather the documents that answer your top twenty questions.
- Set up the new assistant and test it alongside the old tool for a week.
- Switch the website snippet once answers and bookings look right.
- Keep social automation running if it serves a purpose the assistant does not.
P: You do not have to abandon a tool that works. The goal is that every visitor gets a quick answer and a route to a booking, and that you can measure which channel brought each lead.
H2[cost-example-at-three-volumes]: Cost example at three volumes
P: Comparing prices at one volume is misleading. Ask for the total at your current volume, at double, and at ten times, and write the answers in a table. Products with per-seat pricing grow as you add people; products with per-conversation pricing grow as you add traffic; a bring-your-own-key product grows with the AI usage you generate, which you can see and change by choosing a cheaper model. The best choice for you depends on which of those you expect to grow. See [byok versus bundled AI pricing](/blog/byok-vs-bundled-ai-pricing-cost-comparison) for worked examples.
H2[ten-questions-that-expose-the-difference]: Ten questions that expose the difference
OL:
- If nobody is online, what does the visitor experience?
- Can it answer a detailed question about my prices from my own documents, and say when it does not know?
- Can the visitor book a time without leaving the conversation?
- How do I find out a lead arrived, and how fast?
- What happens to a conversation that needs a person: who is told, and what do they see?
- Which channels are included in my plan and which cost extra?
- Is the AI cost included, credit-based or billed to me directly?
- What can I export, and can I leave with my data?
- What is on the roadmap and what is live today?
- Can I see how a real customer conversation reads, unedited?
P: Write the answers in a table for each product. Differences that seemed small in marketing pages often become obvious when set side by side.
H2[signs-you-have-chosen-the-wrong-type-of-tool]: Signs you have chosen the wrong type of tool
UL:
- You bought a live chat suite, and chats sit unanswered because nobody is free.
- You built social flows, and customers keep asking questions the flows cannot answer.
- You bought an AI assistant, but most of your customers only message you on a platform it does not support.
- Your leads are captured but nobody is notified.
- You keep exporting data manually because the tools do not talk to each other.
P: Each of these has a remedy, often a second tool or a different setup, and none is a disaster if you notice in the first month. That is a good reason to use trials and to review real conversations early.
H2[frequently-asked-questions]: Frequently asked questions
FAQ:
Q: What is a good alternative to Tidio for a service business?
A: If you do not have staff to answer chats and you want bookings and leads, consider an AI assistant built for appointments, such as Crescelo. If you want a strong human inbox, a live chat suite may fit better.
Q: Is Crescelo an alternative to ManyChat?
A: Only partly. ManyChat-style tools focus on social platform campaigns. Crescelo focuses on answering website visitors from your content and booking appointments. Some businesses use both.
Q: Does Crescelo work on Instagram or Messenger?
A: No. It works in a website widget, a hosted page and, on the top plan, WhatsApp and Telegram.
Q: Can it replace live chat entirely?
A: It can cover the hours when nobody is online, and your team can take over any conversation when they are available.
Q: What does it cost compared with a live chat suite?
A: It depends on your team size and volume. Crescelo's plans start at $49 a month with no per-seat charges on Starter. Compare the total at your own volume.
Q: Can I try it before switching?
A: Yes. Try the playground with your own documents with no sign-up, then start a free trial.
